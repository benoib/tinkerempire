# TinkerEmpire AI: what we can sell (brainstorm memo)

*Draft, 25 Nov 2026. Brainstorm, not final copy. Prices are starting hypotheses to test, not decisions.*

## 1. The one-line pitch

**An operator, not a consultant.** I run a bootstrapped, profitable AI consumer business and build my own agents every day. I install what already works for us inside your company, and I measure it in money saved or earned.

Most "AI consultants" learned AI in 2023. The difference to sell is 15 years of ML plus a live P&L that depends on AI working.

## 3. Who buys

Ranked by how well the proof bank fits:

1. **B2C / app / DTC founders (1–50 people)** who spend on ads and AI and need the cost under control. Best fit: they are us, a year earlier.
2. **SMBs and agencies** that know they "should do AI" and want one agent that does real work, not a chatbot demo.
3. **Product and engineering teams** that want to ship faster with coding agents and do not know how to set it up properly.
4. **Investors / studios** that need a technical eye on AI startups (due diligence, cost structure).

Geography: French-speaking market (network, language) plus Dubai / GCC (new base, high demand for AI, few real operators).

## 4. Offer ladder

Start small and fixed-price, then grow into builds and retainers.

| # | Offer | What they get | Format | Price hypothesis |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Free AI teardown** (lead magnet) | 30-min call plus a one-page "3 places AI pays in your business" | Free, limited slots | 0 |
| 1 | **AI Opportunity Sprint** | Map workflows and costs, pick 2–3 bets with expected ROI, a build plan you can execute with or without us | 1–2 weeks, fixed | €3–6k |
| 2 | **AI Cost Cut** | Audit of model and GPU spend, then implement the fixes (model choice, caching, batching, routing). The factor-15 story | 2–4 weeks | Fixed fee + % of first-year savings |
| 3 | **Agent Build** | One production agent wired to their tools (CRM, ads, analytics, docs), with human approval before anything that spends money, plus a company wiki the agent reads | 4–8 weeks | €15–40k |
| 4 | **AI Growth Engine** (B2C) | The ROAST machine: AI creative production for ads, programmatic SEO, lifecycle email, tracking | Monthly retainer | €5–10k/month |
| 5 | **Ship-with-AI enablement** | Claude Code / Codex setup, skills, review loops, knowledge base for the team, then a workshop | 1–3 days + setup | €2–5k/day |
| 6 | **Fractional AI CTO / advisor** | A few hours a week: architecture, hiring, vendor choice, cost reviews | Retainer | €3–6k/month |

Recommended starting point: **lead with #1 and #2**. They are short, easy to say yes to, and naturally lead to #3 or #4. #2 is the most differentiated: few people can credibly promise a cost cut.

## 6. Open questions (to decide before launching)

1. **Capacity.** How many hours a week, next to ROAST and Nick AI? This decides whether we sell builds (#3, #4) or only sprints and advice.
2. **"We".** Solo, or with partners/freelancers for delivery? Who?
3. **Productized or bespoke?** Fixed packages scale better and sell easier; bespoke pays more per deal.
4. **Brand.** Sell under TinkerEmpire (studio umbrella) or under Benoît's personal name (LinkedIn / Overthinkinggrowth audience)? Probably personal name for trust, TinkerEmpire as the entity and invoice.
5. **Exclusions.** Which clients or sectors do we say no to (competitors of ROAST/Nick, regulated finance, etc.)?
6. **Language and market.** French first, English first, or both from day one?
